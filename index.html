Our Passion for Perfection

Crafting Pakistan's Finest Hand-Cut Loaded Potatoes & Smashed Patties

FryNation was founded with a single, unapologetic mission: elevate Pakistani fast food. Inspired by traditional Belgian street friteries and American retro smashed burger joints, we refused to serve frozen, mass-produced bagged fries.

Every morning in our central commissary kitchens, we inspect high-starch Russet potatoes grown in Punjab, hand-cut them into generous thick-cut batons, soak them to extract excess starch, and triple-fry them for that legendary crunch outside and fluffy cloud inside. Topped with our proprietary garlic mayo and aged melted cheddar, it is the comfort food experience Pakistan deserves.
